Understand staking

Put the mechanics first.

Staking may involve delegating or locking supported tokens to help secure a proof-of-stake network. Rewards and risks depend on the network and service model.

  • Review validator and protocol risks.
  • Check lockup and unbonding periods.
  • Understand fees and reward variability.
  • Never stake funds you cannot afford to lose.
